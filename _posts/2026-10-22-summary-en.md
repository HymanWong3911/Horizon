## [OpenAI Models Are Coming to Google Cloud](https://openai.com/index/openai-models-google-cloud/) ⭐️ 4.0/10

OpenAI models will be offered through Google Cloud, enabling enterprise customers to purchase and use them while drawing down existing Google Cloud commitments. Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner despite the expanded Google Cloud distribution. The move makes OpenAI easier to adopt for enterprises already committed to Google Cloud and intensifies competition with Anthropic, Azure, and other enterprise AI platforms. It also shows OpenAI broadening distribution beyond its Microsoft-centric infrastructure without changing its core cloud partnership. A key commercial detail is that eligible Google Cloud spend commitments can be applied to OpenAI model purchases. The announcement is primarily an enterprise distribution and procurement arrangement, not a replacement of Microsoft as OpenAI's primary cloud infrastructure provider.

hackernews · armcat · Oct 21, 12:53 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50180079)

**Background**: Cloud commitments are negotiated spending agreements in which enterprises commit to consume a certain amount of cloud services over time. Allowing those commitments to be used for third-party AI models can simplify procurement for large customers. Major cloud providers increasingly distribute models from multiple AI vendors, while Anthropic has already made its models available through Google Cloud, AWS, and Azure.

**Discussion**: Commentators focused on the strategic tension in the announcement, especially the disclaimer that Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner. Some argued Google is prioritizing profitable cloud distribution and TPU sales over giving Gemini or DeepMind exclusive support, while others noted that allowing customers to draw down existing GCP commitments could reduce previously reported revenue. Several saw the move as OpenAI catching up to Anthropic's multi-cloud availability, with one commenter making a joking reference to Oracle.
