## [Is AI killing self-help nonfiction books?](https://tim.blog/2026/06/12/has-ai-already-killed-nonfiction/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A Hacker News discussion sparked by a June 12, 2026 Tim Ferriss blog post explores whether large language models have made traditional self-help nonfiction books obsolete, with most participants agreeing LLMs can extract core actionable information far more efficiently without the filler content common in these books. This discussion reveals a clear shift in user content consumption preferences toward no-filler, immediately actionable information, which carries important reference value for content creators, publishing industry practitioners, and operators providing personal IP services. Participants also noted critical limitations of LLMs for this use case, including high risk of hallucinations, sycophancy, and shallow, misleading explanations when users seek advice in a domain they have no existing familiarity with.

hackernews · imakwana · Jun 16, 17:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48558489)

**Background**: A large language model (LLM) is an advanced AI system built on deep neural networks, trained on massive volumes of public text data to perform tasks including text generation, summarization, analysis and contextualization. LLMs have become widely accessible to general users in recent years, and are increasingly being used to process existing published content to extract targeted information without reading full original works.
