## [Moebius 0.2B Inpainting Model Hits 10B-Level Performance](https://hustvl.github.io/Moebius/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The newly released Moebius lightweight image inpainting model with only 226 million parameters delivers performance comparable to 10B-level large models, and the related Hacker News thread shares user-built browser demos and real-world test feedback from early users. This model breaks the previous narrative that high-quality image inpainting requires heavy, expensive large model computing resources, making high-performance inpainting accessible on consumer-grade devices and edge hardware for creators and marketing content producers. Moebius has only 2% of the parameter count of the 11.9B FLUX.1-Fill-Dev model, outputs 512x512 resolution images, and some early testers noted that inpainted regions are visibly smoother than surrounding areas and it performs poorly on novel objects.

hackernews · DSemba · Jun 22, 13:53 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48630171)

**Background**: AI image inpainting is a technology that can remove unwanted objects, replace existing elements or add new content to specified areas of an image. Traditional high-performance inpainting models usually have over 10 billion parameters, which require powerful dedicated GPU hardware to run and are hard to deploy on regular consumer devices. The Moebius model, developed by the Hust Vision Lab, is a task-specific lightweight framework that will be presented at ECCV 2026.

Some users also asked for basic definitions of image inpainting and reported that existing Hugging Face demo spaces failed to process most test images.
