## [OpenAI Launches GPT-6.1 Terra for Visual Work and Computer Use](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/26/gpt-61-terra/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast and efficient mid-tier model that improves visual reasoning, computer use, natural writing, and video editing over GPT-5.6 Terra. It is available through the API as gpt-6.1-terra and is priced at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. The lower-cost midrange positioning makes GPT-6.1 Terra relevant for high-volume, repetitive computer-use and workplace automation tasks where calling a flagship model would be expensive. Its release also intensifies competition around agentic models and multimodal productivity tools, including offerings compared with Anthropic's Sonnet line. OpenAI's Ted Sanders described Terra as stronger than Astra and Sol at technical communication, similar on vision and computer use but much faster, and weaker on most other tasks. A maximum-reasoning pelican test cost 11.3161 cents and took 4 minutes 11 seconds, while Terra's API price is about half that of GPT-6.1 Sol and roughly ten times that of GPT-6 Luna.

rss · Simon Willison · Oct 26, 23:53

**Background**: GPT-6.1 Terra occupies a middle position in OpenAI's model lineup, alongside smaller Luna and more capable Sol or Astra models. Computer use refers to a model's ability to interpret screen content and operate graphical interfaces, while visual reasoning involves multi-step analysis of images, diagrams, technical drawings, or video. Mid-tier models are intended to balance intelligence, speed, and cost for everyday agentic, coding, writing, and office workflows.
